# Risks, decisions and open questions

Keep this file current. Add a dated line whenever a decision is made or a risk changes.

## Risk register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Effect | What we do about it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The UAE has too little propeller traffic for the method | high | most UAE uploads end as not locatable | coverage probe first (M0); show coverage plainly; keep the New York pack as the working demo |
| 2 | Short clips rarely contain two or more flyovers | high | few real cases are searchable | spectrogram in seconds so the analyst can see for themselves; set expectations on the upload screen; long recordings are the target |
| 3 | Two flyovers presented as a location | medium | a wrong place reported as fact | state rules in `06-confidence.md`; Located needs three flyovers and a located verdict |
| 4 | Wrong air temperature | high in the UAE | kilometres of error (measured) | temperature is a required input; two-temperature runs for wide windows; shown in the report |
| 5 | GPS jamming or spoofing corrupts flight tracks | medium | wrong modelled curves | integrity filter; show share of dropped points; cap the band |
| 6 | Automatic detection proposes vehicles, trains, machinery | high | false flyovers bury the truth | burst flag; analyst confirms every flyover; only confirmed events are used |
| 7 | Edited or dropped-frame recordings | medium | events on different clocks | advise uncut originals; later, detect cuts and split |
| 8 | Wind is not modelled | medium | residual error, weaker scores | later: wind from a weather archive |
| 9 | dopplertrack changes or breaks | low | rework | pinned commit; smoke test |
| 10 | Windows or macOS development: no fork, one worker | high | slow searches, or crashes if workers > 1 on Windows | `workers=1` off Linux; Docker for the worker |
| 11 | Day fetch is a 4 GB stream | certain | minutes of waiting, bandwidth | pre-fetch chosen days; "data not loaded" state; ask the user before bulk fetching |
| 12 | The reference recording cannot be obtained | medium | no verified real demo | synthetic demo; ask the user about the YouTube download |
| 13 | Misuse to locate private people | inherent | harm | accounts, case reference, audit log, rate limits, no public endpoint, stated intended use |
| 14 | Verdict has too few wrong pairs in a small region | high in the UAE | `insufficient`, no confidence | search the whole pack; placebo-day control (M5) |
| 15 | Hackathon time runs out | medium | unfinished UI | cut lines in `07-build-plan.md`; core first |
| 16 | Helicopters pass the prop filter | medium | detections outside `detect`'s speed range, odd matches | count them in the probe; consider excluding by type until tested |
| 17 | Track glitches put kinks in modelled curves | medium | true pair loses to a smooth wrong one (seen on the railcam) | integrity filter helps; smoothing is future work upstream |
| 18 | Automatic detection is slow: 10 s per minute of audio with library defaults, 2 s with our fast settings, on one core (measured) | certain | proposals arrive after the analyst reaches the editor on long files | spectrogram first; sliced detection streaming proposals; the editor works without them; more cores on Linux |
| 19 | Detection misplaces or misses flyovers (measured: top proposals on an approach tone, a real one 23 s late and 16 Hz low) | high | analyst trusts a bad proposal | proposals are hints, never auto-confirmed; the search always refits the analyst's box |
| 20 | `fit_event` raises on a narrow box and is slow on a big one (measured) | certain without the fix | editor errors, 10 to 19 s waits | widening rule; minimum box size; quick preview in the editor, full fit in the search job |
| 21 | Long recordings use a lot of memory (about 115 MB per hour while decoding, 48 MB per hour of spectrogram plus working copies) | medium | worker killed on small machines | `MAX_DURATION_H=6` to start; memory-mapped spectrogram; measure on a real long file |
| 22 | The quick preview and the full fit can disagree (one of nine comparisons) | low | curve in the editor differs from the one on the result | the search uses the full fit; the result draws the search's curves |
